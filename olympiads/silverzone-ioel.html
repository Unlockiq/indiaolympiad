SilverZone iOEL — International Olympiad of English Language

SilverZone Foundation · Classes 1–12 · 2026–27 window: Oct – Dec 2026 (expected)

SilverZone's English language olympiad.

Comparable ground to SOF IEO; check silverzone.org for this cycle's dates.

Who is it for?

Students of Classes 1–12. Subject focus: English. As with any olympiad, the best candidates are children who voluntarily enjoy the subject — an olympiad should stretch curiosity, not manufacture pressure.

Registration & source of record

Official information and registration: silverzone.org. Always verify the current date, fee and eligibility on the organiser's own site before paying — organisers occasionally shift windows, and this page is a directory entry, not the organiser.
