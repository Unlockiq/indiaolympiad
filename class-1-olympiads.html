Olympiads for Class 1

16 exams open to Class 1 this season — 11 with organiser-confirmed dates. Honest guidance first, list second.

At Classes 1–2, an olympiad should feel like a puzzle day, not an exam. Pick at most one, favour formats with pictures and short papers, and treat the certificate as the celebration it is. If your child hesitates, skip the year — nothing is lost.
