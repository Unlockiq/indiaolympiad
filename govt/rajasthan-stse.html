Rajasthan State Talent Search Examination (STSE) 2026

राज्य स्तरीय प्रतिभा खोज परीक्षा

Conducted by Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (BSER), Ajmer. Classes 10 and 12 (Science / Commerce / Arts) · MAT · Language (LCT) · Scholastic (SAT).

Govt · StateClasses 10 and 12 (Science / Commerce / Arts)Verified 31 October 2026

Key dates

ExamSun 22 Nov 2026 · 9:00 AM–1:00 PM · all 41 district HQs Official
RegistrationClosed · 15–24 Sep 2026 (late fee till 28 Sep) Official
ResultExpected Jan 2027 (2025 cycle result ~4 Jan 2026) Expected
Fee₹300 general; ₹175 SC/ST/BPL/divyang/CWSN (certificate upload mandatory); +₹50 late; schools may collect ₹20 forwarding fee.

Source for the exam date: BSER notice सैल-7/STSE-2026/01 dated 14 Sep 2026 and STSE-2026 instruction booklet.

Eligibility

Regular students of Class 10 and Class 12 in any recognised school in Rajasthan — central, public, Navodaya, convent, private, government and RMSA model schools — with at least 50% in Class 9 (for Class 10) or Class 11 (for Class 12).

Who can sit vs who gets the money: Open to all boards. Scholarship money is restricted to students of government schools and Rajasthan model schools; others receive certificates and the top-20 cash incentives.

Paper pattern

PaperContentMarks · time
Session 1 · 9:00–9:45Mental Ability Test (MAT)50 Q · 50 marks · 45 min
Session 2 · 10:15–11:00Language Comprehension Test (LCT) — Hindi or English40 Q · 40 marks · 45 min
Session 3 · 11:30–1:00Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) — Class 10: Phy 25, Chem 25, Bio 20, Maths 20; Class 12: stream-wise 30/30/30 (Arts 23/23/23/21)90 Q · 90 marks · 90 min

All MCQ, 1 mark each, total 180; no negative marking; merit on MAT+LCT+SAT total; extra time for CWSN candidates. Syllabus: full previous-class syllabus (2025-26) plus current-year syllabus (2026-27).

Medium

Hindi and English.

Scholarship

Top 50 per category (Class 10; Class 12 Science; Class 12 Arts & Commerce) scoring ≥80%: ₹1,250/month in Classes 11–12 and ₹2,000/month through regular UG/PG study (government and model-school students). Incentives for the top 20 in each merit list: ₹4,000 (rank 1) and ₹2,000 (ranks 2–20). Scholar certificate >90%, Distinction certificate 80–90%.

Online by the school through bser-exam.in/stse using the Board login; fee by ICICI Bank or Rajasthan Gramin Bank challan; hard copies posted to the Board.
